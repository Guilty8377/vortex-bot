              Terms of use

              Vortex is a paper trading website made for a school business assignment. All trades use pretend money and no real cryptocurrency is ever bought, sold or held.

              Nothing on Vortex is financial advice. Calls, safety scores and KOL wallets are shown to help you learn and can be wrong. Real memecoin trading is very risky and most new tokens lose almost all their value.

              Token data comes from public third party sources and may be late, missing or wrong.

              Privacy

              Vortex has no accounts and no server. Your balance, trades, settings and help requests are saved only in your own browser and are deleted if you clear your browser data or reset your wallet.

              To show live data your browser connects directly to PumpPortal, DexScreener and Jupiter, and loads fonts from Google Fonts and the chart from unpkg. These services can see your IP address, the same as visiting their websites.
